# Decisions Log

Each entry records a decision point hit during the autonomous build, the option chosen, and a one-line rationale. Numbered in the order they were made.

1. **Project location.** Built in a new subdirectory `/Users/michael/intel/optiv-fairr-practice` rather than the working directory root, because the root already holds an unrelated project (`optiv-intel-server`, `index.html`, `node_modules`) and the repo must contain only this practice.
2. **Compensation sources blocked from automated fetch.** Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, and 6figr return bot-check pages; figures were captured from the search index snapshot and flagged for browser re-verification in `research/sources/16-*.md`. Salary.com and BLS fetched directly. Kept the $265K loaded default because Glassdoor total pay (~$262K average) plus the BLS 30.1% benefits load bounds it.
3. **Optiv "Elite" tier.** The public Optiv partner page does not use the word "Elite"; it cites Partner of the Year awards and $1B joint sales. The plan uses the author-stated Elite status but cites the public figures for external claims.
4. **Blended rate not changed.** Research bounds $325/hr at the top of the general security band and inside the AppSec/vCISO bands; kept the default and recorded the bounds in `model/ASSUMPTIONS.md`.
5. **Delivery hours per offering.** Derived from price divided by the $325 blended rate and rounded (Assessment 260h, Sprint 430h, Retainer tiers 68/117/200 h per month, Board Readout 74h, Falcon Complete add-on 6 advisor-hours per customer-month). The add-on is delivered mostly by the existing Falcon Complete/Threat Management team, so it carries an internal delivery-cost transfer instead of full advisor hours.
6. **Falcon Flex program margin interpretation.** The brief's "10% partner program margin uplift on the CrowdStrike side" is modeled conservatively as CrowdStrike retaining 10% of Flex-transacted contract value; Optiv's P&L uses net revenue. The input can be set to zero or negative to model a net incentive to Optiv. Labeled ASSUMPTION.
7. **Utilization ramp applied by advisor tenure.** The 35/55/70/75 ramp applies to each advisor from their own start month (founders from month 1, hires from arrival), so new hires do not appear instantly productive.
8. **Hiring trigger is inclusive at the threshold.** Because steady-state utilization is capped at 75% by the ramp, the trigger "exceeds 75%" can never fire under a strict inequality; the rule is implemented as trailing-3-month utilization at or above 75% AND backlog above 8 weeks.
9. **Hiring lag and cooldown.** Added a 2-month recruiting lag and a 3-month cooldown after each hire decision so a single backlog spike does not fire the rule every month while the first hire is still ramping. Both are editable inputs.
10. **Backlog and lost demand.** Signed work that exceeds capacity queues as backlog; when backlog at the start of a month exceeds 12 weeks, new assessments that month are declined (lost), and their expected lifetime value is reported as revenue left on the table. This keeps backlog finite and makes the capacity constraint visible.
11. **Internal bench for surge.** Added an internal bench (Optiv AppSec and Cyber Strategy consultants) of up to 240 hours per month from month 4 at a $165/hr internal transfer cost, engaged only when backlog exceeds 2 weeks. Labeled ASSUMPTION; defaults are editable and can be set to zero.
12. **Revenue recognition under capacity constraint.** Revenue is recognized on delivered hours, pro-rata across offerings from a single work pool. Fixed-fee retainers therefore slip rather than bill for undelivered work, which is conservative.
13. **Price escalator.** The 4% annual escalator applies to all billings in a given practice year (new deals through the price list, retainers through a contractual escalator clause). This keeps the TypeScript model and the Excel formulas identical.
14. **Payment terms in cash flow.** Net 45 on month-end invoicing is modeled as 50% of each month's revenue collected in the following month and 50% in the month after.
15. **Break-even definitions.** "Break-even month" is the first month cumulative cash returns to zero or above (cash break-even). The first month of sustained positive EBITDA is reported separately.
16. **Board readout attach.** The 40% attach applies to completed Prioritization Sprints (delivered the month after the sprint) and to each retainer renewal (annual attestation refresh).
17. **Downside scenario floor.** "One fewer assessment per month" is applied with a floor of zero, so months 1 to 3 have no assessment intake in the Downside case.
18. **Tornado method.** Each candidate input is flexed plus and minus 20% of its base value (attach rates and utilization by plus/minus 10 percentage points), and the ten inputs with the largest EBITDA Year 2 swing are shown.
19. **Excel formulas verified without Excel.** LibreOffice is not installed on the build machine, so the workbook's live formulas are recalculated with the HyperFormula engine in the validation script and compared against the TypeScript model.
