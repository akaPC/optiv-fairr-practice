## Structural assumptions (not single inputs)

- Fixed-fee revenue is recognized ratably on delivered effort: the Assessment inside its month, the Sprint two-thirds in its first month and one-third in its second, retainers monthly, the Board Readout in its delivery month. See `docs/DECISIONS.md` #12.
- The Sprint starts the month after the Assessment closes; the Retainer starts the month after the Sprint ends; renewals occur twelve months after each retainer start at the renewal rate.
- Signed work beyond capacity queues as backlog; new assessments are declined while the backlog exceeds the cap, and their expected 36-month value is reported as revenue left on the table (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #10).
- The Falcon Complete add-on is delivered mainly by the existing Falcon Complete and Threat Management teams; advisors contribute oversight hours that take first call on capacity, and a delivery-cost transfer is charged against add-on revenue (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #5).
- The annual price escalator applies to all billings by practice year (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #13).
- Net-45 payment terms collect half of each month's revenue in the following month and half in the month after (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #14).
- The Falcon Flex program margin reduces net revenue on the Flex-transacted share of deals; CrowdStrike has not published partner economics (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #6).
