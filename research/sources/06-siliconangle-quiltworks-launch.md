## Cleaned extract

Coalition members at launch: CrowdStrike (lead), Accenture, Ernst & Young, IBM's Cybersecurity Services, Kroll, OpenAI, with frontier-model support from OpenAI and Anthropic. Launch offering: Frontier AI Readiness and Resilience Service, a 12-month renewable subscription via Falcon Flex dollars, covering security assessments, AI-powered code scanning, red-team prioritization, and board-level reporting.

The article's framing of why the trigger matters: frontier models uncover vulnerability classes (logic bugs, design flaws, misconfigurations, novel exploit paths) that traditional tools miss, and are "collapsing the window defenders have between discovery and exploitation." CrowdStrike's approach moves organizations past CVSS-based triage by using adversary intelligence and attack-path analysis to rank which vulnerabilities an attacker can actually reach.

On the SI role: launch partners frame their involvement as operational capacity. Accenture's Harpreet Sidhu emphasized delivering "the operational muscle to remediate code-level issues"; the coalition taps CrowdStrike's network of "more than 10,000 certified professionals." IBM's Mark Hughes tied it to IBM's "Autonomous Security approach."
