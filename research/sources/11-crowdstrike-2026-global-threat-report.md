# 2026 CrowdStrike Global Threat Report: AI Accelerates Adversaries and Reshapes the Attack Surface

- **URL:** https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/2026-crowdstrike-global-threat-report/
- **Publisher:** CrowdStrike (press release for the annual report)
- **Date:** February 24, 2026
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-04

## Quantitative findings extracted

- Attacks by AI-enabled adversaries increased 89% year-over-year.
- Average eCrime breakout time fell to 29 minutes (65% faster than 2024).
- Fastest observed breakout time: 27 seconds.
- Data exfiltration began within 4 minutes of initial access in one intrusion.
- Adversaries exploited GenAI tools at more than 90 organizations.
- 42% increase in zero-day vulnerabilities exploited before public disclosure (as cited on the QuiltWorks program page and the CrowdStrike frontier AI blog).
- Cloud-conscious intrusions rose 37%; state-nexus cloud targeting up 266%.
- China-nexus activity increased 38%; 67% of China-nexus exploited vulnerabilities delivered immediate system access; 40% targeted internet-facing edge devices.
- DPRK-linked incidents rose more than 130%.
