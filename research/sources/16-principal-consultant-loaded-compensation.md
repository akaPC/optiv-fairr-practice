# Loaded compensation for principal-level security consultants (U.S.)

- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-04
- **Note on retrieval:** Glassdoor and ZipRecruiter serve bot-check pages to automated fetchers; the figures below were captured from the search-engine index of those pages on 2026-09-04 and should be re-verified in a browser before external use. Salary.com and BLS were fetched directly.

## Source A: Glassdoor, "Principal Security Consultant Salaries" (United States)
- **URL:** https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/principal-security-consultant-salary-SRCH_KO0,29.htm
- **As of:** May 2026 (per index snapshot)
- Average total pay: $261,595 per year. Typical range $204,243 (25th percentile) to $341,447 (75th percentile); 90th percentile reported up to $428,997.
- Related title, "Principal Cyber Security Consultant" (June 2026): average $232,893; range $190,160 to $289,619.

## Source B: ZipRecruiter, "Principal Security Consultant Salary"
- **URL:** https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Principal-Security-Consultant-Salary
- **As of:** May 2026 (per index snapshot)
- Average annual pay $140,048; 25th percentile $113,000; 75th percentile $171,000; 90th percentile $194,000. (ZipRecruiter's sample skews to posted-job salaries rather than reported total compensation, which explains the gap to Glassdoor.)

## Source C: Salary.com, "Principal Security Analyst Salary" (United States)
- **URL:** https://www.salary.com/research/salary/listing/principal-security-analyst-salary
- **As of:** September 1, 2026
- Average base salary $122,887; 25th percentile $114,033; 75th percentile $133,860; 90th percentile $143,850. Base only.

## Source D: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employer Costs for Employee Compensation, March 2026
- **URL:** https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm
- **Released:** June 12, 2026
- Private industry: total compensation $46.60/hour worked; wages and salaries $32.60 (69.9%); benefits $14.01 (30.1%). Benefits at the 90th wage percentile: $29.31/hour.

## How the plan uses this
A Principal Advisor at Optiv is a senior, client-facing, CrowdStrike-certified practitioner; the relevant comparator is Glassdoor's Principal Security Consultant total pay (~$262K average) rather than ZipRecruiter's job-posting average. Applying the BLS private-industry benefits load (30.1% of wages, equivalently benefits = 43% of wages-and-salaries) to a base-plus-bonus of roughly $185K to $200K yields a fully loaded cost in the $265K range, which is the model default. The default is therefore consistent with, but not derived line-by-line from, these sources; it remains an editable input.
