## Cleaned extract

Amanda Adams (CrowdStrike SVP Global Alliances) on QuiltWorks and Falcon IQ: "QuiltWorks is a coalition of folks and partners who join us; they leverage our platform... this is a tool built on AWS that allows a partner to essentially drive an assessment and highlight the opportunities, the priorities." Falcon IQ is described as an AWS-built tool intended to compress remediation timelines from discovery to resolution.

Scale metrics reported: 900+ technology integrations in CrowdStrike's partner ecosystem; channel partners "building new revenue streams" on the integrated stack. Mona Chadha (AWS) on guardrails: "Security is job zero... ensure that these agents don't go out of that sandbox."
