## Cleaned extract

Project QuiltWorks is CrowdStrike's industry coalition for frontier AI readiness. Powered by frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic, it brings together the technology, expertise, and ecosystem needed to close the AI vulnerability gap for organizations of every size. "Answer the only questions that matter: Are we protected? Are we ready for frontier AI risk?"

**Partner categories (as listed on the page at retrieval):**
- Frontier AI model partners: OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA. "Leading AI labs power vulnerability discovery, scanning code and infrastructure to surface risks before adversaries can exploit them."
- Global systems integrators: Accenture, EY, IBM, Kroll, Cognizant, HCLTech, Infosys, KPMG, NTT DATA, Tata Consultancy Services, Wipro, Armadin. "A network of 10,000+ certified professionals delivers enterprise-scale remediation, risk assessment, and board-level reporting across industries and regions."
- Cyber insurance & risk partners: Coalition, Liberty Mutual, Lockton, Resilience, Marsh. "Underwriters and brokers quantify financial exposure to AI risk and deliver coordinated coverage and mitigation strategies."
- SMB & channel partners: Arrow, Ignition, Nord Security, Pax8, TD Synnex, Westcon Comstor, Zip.
- Technology alliance partners: AWS, Abnormal AI, Artemis, AttackIQ, ExtraHop, HackerOne, Horizon3, Netskope, Picus Security, Rubrik, SafeBreach, Terra, Zscaler.

**Falcon IQ:** "The new operational engine turns Project QuiltWorks expertise into AI-driven security action."

**Market framing (page copy, citing the CrowdStrike 2026 Global Threat Report):** "The window between vulnerability discovery and exploitation has collapsed from weeks to minutes." Stats tiles reference the increase in attacks by AI-enabled adversaries, the fastest adversary breakout time in 2025, and the increase in zero-day vulnerabilities exploited prior to public disclosure. FAQ: "In 2025 alone, more than 48,000 vulnerabilities were documented as CVEs, a 20% year-over-year increase. Legacy vulnerability management cannot keep up: periodic scans produce more findings than teams can process, CVSS severity scores don't reflect what's actually exploitable in a given environment, and patch cycles measured in weeks are no match for exploits measured in hours."

**Four-step process ("Close the AI vulnerability gap, together"):**
1. Expert review of your current program, where you stand today, and your capacity to remediate.
2. Frontier AI-powered scanning of applications and codebases to find exploitable vulnerabilities that legacy tools and manual review miss.
3. Expert red teamers rank findings by exploitability, adversary activity, and business impact – beyond CVSS scores.
4. Guided remediation through the partner platform turns findings into action. Board-level analysis and read-outs give leadership clear answers, not raw data.

**FAQ:** "Project QuiltWorks is neither a product nor a single service. It's a CrowdStrike-led industry coalition that unites frontier AI leaders, global systems integrators, distributors, MSPs, MSSPs, channel partners, and the Falcon platform..." Calls to action: "Assess my exposures", "Partner with us", "Start free trial" (15-day). Linked resources: "Five Steps for Frontier AI Security Readiness"; "CrowdStrike Frontier AI Readiness and Resilience Service" datasheet.
