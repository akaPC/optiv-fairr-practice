# CrowdStrike Expands Project QuiltWorks Across the Tech Ecosystem, Uniting More Data Sources to Secure Frontier AI Risk

- **URL:** https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-expands-project-quiltworks-across-tech-ecosystem-to-secure-frontier-ai-risk/
- **Publisher:** CrowdStrike (press release)
- **Dateline:** AUSTIN, Texas and Fal.Con 2026, Las Vegas – August 31, 2026
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-04

## Cleaned extract

CrowdStrike integrated real-time data from twelve technology partners into Falcon Next-Gen SIEM through Project QuiltWorks: Abnormal AI, Artemis Security, AttackIQ, ExtraHop, HackerOne, Horizon3, Netskope, Picus Security, Rubrik, SafeBreach, Terra Security, and Zscaler.

Core components described: Falcon Onum real-time data pipelines (CrowdStrike states storage cost reductions of up to 50 percent); Falcon IQ correlates customer telemetry with threat intelligence; over 50 pre-built agents automate workflows; powered by frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and NVIDIA Nemotron.

Daniel Bernard (CBO) framed the announcement around cybersecurity being "a data problem" and frontier models making "the clock shorter" for exploitation.
