# CrowdStrike Launches Flex for Services, Bringing Flexible Cybersecurity Services Consumption to the Agentic Era

- **URL:** https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-launches-flex-for-services-bringing-flexible-cybersecurity-services-consumption-to-the-agentic-era/
- **Publisher:** CrowdStrike (press release, RSA 2026)
- **Date:** March 24, 2026
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-04

## Cleaned extract

CrowdStrike extended the Falcon Flex licensing model to its complete services portfolio (Flex for Services) and introduced the Zero Dollar Flex Fund. Flex for Services operates independently from platform subscriptions and covers threat hunting, red teaming, readiness assessments, AI advisory, and incident response through flexible hour pools aligned with organizational risk. Flex ending ARR was $1.69 billion in Q4 FY26, growing over 120% year-over-year.

Partner participation: "Flex for Services unlocks new growth for CrowdStrike's global ecosystem of system integrators, managed service providers, and incident response partners." Robb Mayeski (Kroll) is quoted on Flex for Services enabling Kroll to scale. No partner economics, margins, or referral terms are disclosed.

Zero Dollar Flex Fund: qualifying new services customers receive 200 hours of CrowdStrike Services (160 incident response, 40 proactive) with no upfront cost.

## What it contributes to the plan
Establishes that services (including "readiness assessments" and "AI advisory") are purchasable with Flex dollars and that SIs participate, but confirms that partner economics are not public. This is why the Flex program-margin input in the model is labeled ASSUMPTION.
