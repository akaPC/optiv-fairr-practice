Notes from the guide: "Security consultants average $185–$325 per hour." "Consulting firms mark up contractor labor by 2.0–3.0x on the bill rate." "Rates rose 6–9% year-over-year, driven largely by AI talent absorption."

## How the plan uses this
The FAIRR blended rate of $325/hr sits at the top of the "security consultants average $185–$325" band (Source A) and inside the AppSec ($200–$425) and vCISO ($250–$450) bands (Source B), which is appropriate for principal-level, CrowdStrike-native remediation advisory delivered by a national integrator. The $325 figure is a pricing assumption, not a survey result; these sources bound it.
