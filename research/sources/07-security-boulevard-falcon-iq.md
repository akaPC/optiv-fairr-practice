# Security Boulevard: CrowdStrike Looks to Operationalize Project QuiltWorks AI Initiative

## Cleaned extract

Falcon IQ, unveiled at Fal.Con 2026, is designed to identify, prioritize, and remediate vulnerabilities using AI and data from security partners. It combines NVIDIA Nemotron models with frontier AI systems, CrowdStrike telemetry, and threat intelligence to analyze risk and generate remediation playbooks at machine speed. Falcon IQ correlates customer telemetry, threat intelligence, and OverWatch findings to generate deployable playbooks and assess organizational risk levels.

Daniel Bernard is quoted describing Falcon IQ as "the method for productizing the Project QuiltWorks methodology based on the NVIDIA Nemotron AI model."

Numbers reported: Project QuiltWorks now includes 20 partners after 12 new technology members joined; CrowdStrike cites $400 million added to sales pipeline attributed to the ecosystem approach; 50+ Charlotte AI agents available.

Author's analysis: the partner economics are an ecosystem play where vendors gain pipeline attribution by feeding data into Falcon, and the core bottleneck Falcon IQ targets is that manual vulnerability remediation cannot keep pace with AI-discovered vulnerabilities.
